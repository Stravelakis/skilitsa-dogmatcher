## Skilitsa DogMatcher, like you're five

You answer some questions about your life, like "do you like long walks?" and "do you have small kids?"

Then the quiz looks at lots of dog breeds and finds the three that would be happiest living with you. It tells you why, in a few words, like a friend who knows dogs.

The grown-up who runs the website keeps all the dog facts in a big table, and the quiz reads that table.
